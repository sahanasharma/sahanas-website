I’m studying Mathematics AA HL, Economics HL and Computer Science HL at Westbourne College Singapore. I’m drawn to problems that need more than one way of thinking.

As a Co-Founder of Solivia Pte. Ltd., I built the company website, conduct market and commercial research, and support financial management. That work motivates my passion project: a transparent commercial decision-support system.

With Algoverse, I work on machine-learning research involving sequence data, high-dimensional embeddings, model evaluation and attention-based approaches. My professional exposure also includes data operations and job shadowing across Pinterest, TikTok and Snapchat.
